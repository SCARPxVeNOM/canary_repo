Connect a wallet or watch an address.

Leaky mode: reads go from your browser to rpc.provider.localhost. Connecting sends your address to tracker.localhost as an event, a pixel and a base64 "uid". /api/points (first party) gets it too.

Native mode: LeakyDash routes every read through anon-rpc (tor-js, in a sandbox) to a public Sepolia endpoint, and ships without the trackers.
