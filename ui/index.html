The Four Moves

  1. Declare

    Dapp publishes a privacy manifest in its ENSv2 name.

  2. Observe

    Scanner loads the dapp with a canary address and records leaks.

  3. Enforce

    Route reads through anon-rpc and block third-party requests.

  4. Measure

    Show coverage: protected, blocked, left exposed, not inspected.

How a read travels

Privacy manifest

  • Who gets your address
  • Why (purpose)
  • Signed & verifiable

The dapp itself can always see your address and your IP. Canary keeps them from third parties: RPC providers, analytics, trackers.

Not inspected: service workers, WebRTC, binary bodies, hashed or encrypted payloads. “Nothing observed” is not “nothing sent”.
