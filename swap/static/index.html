    How it works
    
      
      
        
    • Quote: quoteExactInputSingle on the v4 Quoter, over eth_call. The Quoter runs the swap against the pool and reverts with the result, so nothing is sent and the request holds the pool and the amount only.
      • 
        
    • Price: getSlot0 and getLiquidity on StateView, for the price impact.
      • 
        
    • Swap: one Universal Router transaction (V4_SWAP: swap, settle, take) that your wallet signs and sends. Selling CNRY first approves Permit2. Your wallet broadcasts through its own RPC, which Canary can't change; an agent can send through anon-rpc (swap.mjs).
